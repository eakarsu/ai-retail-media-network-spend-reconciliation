# Retail Media Network Spend Reconciliation

Audit retail-media delivery, pricing, attribution, budget, and makegood credits across retailer networks.

**Primary buyer:** Consumer brands and retail media buyers. **Evidence:** media agreements, campaigns, audiences, impressions, clicks, attributed sales, rate cards, budgets, makegoods, invoices, and credits.

Full local application built with React, Vite, Express, PostgreSQL, and OpenRouter. Includes 15 domain-specific capabilities, 105 custom AI workbench fields, three scenario-fill controls per feature, operational registers, workflow transitions, analytics, professional AI decision briefs, audit history, and at least 15 PostgreSQL records per capability.

## Domain capabilities

- Retail media agreement library
- Campaign order ingestion
- Audience targeting evidence
- Impression delivery validation
- Click event reconciliation
- Rate card calculation
- Budget pacing control
- Attributed sales methodology
- Incrementality evidence
- Invalid traffic adjustment
- Under-delivery makegood
- Invoice campaign matching
- Retailer dispute workflow
- Credit reconciliation
- Network campaign analytics
